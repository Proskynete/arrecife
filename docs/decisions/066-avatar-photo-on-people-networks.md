# § 66 · The avatar is his face where people meet him, and Tiburoncín where developers do

**Release:** unreleased · [index](README.md)

---

**System rule:** «Avatar de perfil: nivel 2 en todas las plataformas, idéntico,
sin variantes por red. La coherencia del avatar es lo que hace que te reconozcan
en el timeline.»

**What there was:** neither. Instagram and the LinkedIn banner wore a circuit
diamond that belongs to no level of the system, and LinkedIn wore a photo. The
rule was not being broken in one direction; it had simply never been applied.

**What it is now:**

- **LinkedIn and Instagram: his photo.** The same photo on both.
- **GitHub, X, Discord, npm: the nivel 2 avatar**, Tiburoncín's head in the
  `#3E7CB1` circle, exactly as the manual draws it.
- The circuit diamond is retired everywhere. Where it stood as a logo — the
  LinkedIn banner — the full lockup takes its place, beside the photo of a full
  auditorium that the banner already carries.

**Why the rule gives way.** The manual's argument is recognition in the
timeline, and it holds for the timeline. It does not hold in a conference
hallway. In the interview the author placed himself not on the stage but «el
que está explicando algo con un café», and he goes to conferences to be that
person — CaribeConf, TechSchool, workshops. The people who follow him on
LinkedIn and Instagram are the ones who will walk up to him there, and a whale
shark does not help anyone find a face in a room. On developer platforms nobody
is going to meet him in a hallway, the mascot is the identity, and the rule
stands as written.

It is still one avatar per kind of network, never one per network, so the
consistency the rule was protecting survives inside each group.

**Action in the document:** in § 03 nivel 2, change «Foto de perfil en GitHub,
LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Discord» to «GitHub, X, Discord, npm». In § 12, replace
«nivel 2 en todas las plataformas, idéntico» with the two groups above and the
reason: the photo where people meet him in person, the mascot where they meet
his code.
