# § 67 · Three merch phrases, taken from his mouth and not from a slogan list

**Release:** unreleased · [index](README.md)

---

**System rule:** after § 65 the only approved merch text is the lema, on the
black tee. The sand tee reads «Frase pendiente».

**What there was:** two rounds of candidates that the author rejected. The first
(«No culpes al martillo», «Primero la spec», «La IA es la herramienta») was
either too generic or needed context to be understood. The second was built on
one verb, and three phrases repeating «manejo» read as a poor vocabulary.

**What it is now:** one phrase from his answer to «la IA lo hizo mal», and his
two life philosophies, which already travelled with him (the GitHub README and,
until § 65, the links page):

| Phrase | Piece |
| --- | --- |
| «Entender antes de construir.» | Black tee (unchanged, § 65) |
| «Yo manejo la herramienta. No al revés.» | Die-cut sticker, with the laptop-and-coffee pose |
| «Si no estás dispuesto a darlo todo, no tienes derecho a intentarlo.» | Sand tee, printed on the back |
| «No permitas que las cosas tomen más tiempo del que tienen que tomar.» | Notebook cover |

The second life philosophy is his own sentence with the word order fixed
(«más tiempo del que», not «más del tiempo que»); he approved the change.

**Why:** the rule that came out of the interview is that a phrase is his when he
already said it, not when it sounds like a developer. Each of the four comes from
something he said in his own words, and no two share a verb.

**Action in the document:** in § 11 · Merch y producción, set the die-cut
sticker's art to «Una pose + «Yo manejo la herramienta. No al revés.» + URL al
pie»; set the sand tee to «Mascota grande al pecho + «Si no estás dispuesto a
darlo todo, no tienes derecho a intentarlo.» en la espalda. URL en la manga»;
add a row «Libreta · tapa dura A5 · una tinta · Aleta + «No permitas que las
cosas tomen más tiempo del que tienen que tomar.» + URL».
